What Ameen is

Ameen helps you read and reflect on the Quran each day. It shows verses, translations and short reflections, and can remind you to read.

Ameen is not a source of religious rulings (fatwas). It does not replace a qualified scholar or teacher. It is not a tafsir work.

Reflections in Ameen are Ameen editorial reflections with a named tafsir basis. They are not presented as the tafsir text itself. For the tafsir itself, read the original source.

Sources and attribution

Quran text and translations come from sources that are named and credited in the app. Each verse and reflection shows its source and version. We try to be accurate, but errors can happen. If you find one, use “Report a correction or concern” in the app’s Trust tab or write to ameenapp.help@gmail.com.
