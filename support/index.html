Support

Questions about Ameen, a subscription, or something in the app that looks wrong? Email us and we will reply.

Email

ameenapp.help@gmail.com

Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the Ameen version (shown in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Ameen). A screenshot helps.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the subscription screen in Ameen and tap Restore purchase.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Ameen. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Apple’s guide to cancelling
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all App Store payments, so refunds go through Apple. Request a refund from Apple
My daily reminder did not go off.
Open the Alarm tab in Ameen and check the reminder status. Then check that Ameen is allowed to send alarms and notifications in iOS Settings, and that a Focus mode is not silencing it.
I found a mistake in a verse, translation or reflection.
Open the Trust tab and tap Report a correction or concern, or email us. Tell us the verse reference and what looks wrong.
How do I delete my data?
Your reading progress, saved verses and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them. To delete the subscription records our payment providers hold, email us. The Privacy Policy explains what we need from you.
