This policy explains how the Ameen: Daily Quran Reflection iOS app (“Ameen”, “the app”, “we”) handles information.

The short version

  • Ameen has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your reading progress, saved verses and settings stay on your device.
  • Ameen has no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK of its own. We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your information.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. RevenueCat checks whether you have an active subscription, and Superwall shows the subscription screen. These two services receive limited purchase, device and usage information that is not linked to your identity.
  • Ameen does not use your location services, camera, photos, contacts, health data or microphone.

Information stored on your device

Ameen stores the following on your device:

  • Whether you finished onboarding.
  • Verses you save.
  • Your progress through topics.
  • Your reminder settings, such as whether reminders are on and the time you chose.
  • A small summary of today’s verse for the Home Screen widget.

This information is kept in the app’s local storage and in a shared storage area (an App Group) that only Ameen and its own widget can read. We do not receive it. It is removed when you delete the app.

The app does not send this data to a server. Ameen’s Quran text and reflections are bundled with the app and are read on the device.

Reminders and notifications

If you turn reminders on, Ameen asks for permission to use iOS alarms and notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Ameen does not send push notifications from a server. You can change this permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Widgets

Ameen’s widget shows a verse reference, Arabic text and, for subscribers, a short preview. The app writes this to an App Group (group.com.ameen.daily) on your device, and the widget reads it from there. This data does not leave your device.

Purchases and third parties

Ameen offers an optional paid subscription. Three third parties are involved. They have their own privacy policies, and those policies apply to what they collect.

Apple

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, trials, renewals, cancellation and refunds. We do not receive your payment card details or your Apple ID. Apple’s privacy policy

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to check whether your purchase gives you premium access and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives:

  • your App Store purchase and subscription information, including the App Store receipt
  • device and app information, such as device type, operating system and app version
  • when you last opened the app
  • an anonymous identifier it creates for your copy of the app

Ameen does not ask you to sign in and does not give RevenueCat your name, email address or any other way to identify you. We use this information to unlock premium features, prevent fraud, and see subscription totals. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf and stores it in the United States. RevenueCat’s privacy policy

Superwall

We use Superwall to show the subscription screen at the right moments and to measure how it performs. Superwall receives:

  • identifiers it creates for your copy of the app, and Apple’s identifier for vendors (a device identifier that is the same only across apps from the same developer; it is not the advertising identifier)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, language and region settings, time zone and network type (Wi-Fi or cellular)
  • your approximate location (country, region and city), worked out from your IP address
  • app activity related to the subscription screen, such as when the app was installed and opened, when the subscription screen was shown, and whether you subscribed or closed it
  • your subscription status and purchase information

Superwall does not receive your name or email address from Ameen. We use this information to decide when to show the subscription screen and to see how often it leads to a purchase. Superwall processes it on our behalf, may use it to improve its own service, and says it does not sell it. Superwall uses other companies to store and analyse this data. Superwall’s privacy policy

What we do not collect

Ameen does not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or health data. Ameen does not use Apple’s advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track you.

The App Store privacy label for Ameen lists purchases, identifiers, usage data and approximate location, all as “Data Not Linked to You”. These come only from RevenueCat and Superwall, as described above.

Emailing us

The Trust tab has two email links: “Contact support” and “Report a correction or concern”. Each opens your email app with a message to us that you can edit before sending. “Contact support” fills in your Ameen version, iOS version and iPhone model, so we can help faster. You can delete these before you send. If you send a message, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems or content.

How long we keep information

We do not run our own servers for the app. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer them. RevenueCat and Superwall keep the information described above while we use their services, and delete it under their own retention rules after a deletion request or when we stop using them. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete the app to remove its local data.
  • Turn off reminders and notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings.
  • To ask us to delete the information RevenueCat and Superwall hold for your copy of the app, email ameenapp.help@gmail.com. Because Ameen has no accounts, we may need your help to find the right record, for example the date and approximate time of a purchase. Superwall completes deletion requests 25 days after we submit them. Payment records Apple keeps are not affected.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights too, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. To use any of these rights, email us. If you are not satisfied with our answer to a privacy complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Children

Ameen is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International transfers

RevenueCat stores data in the United States. Superwall and the companies it uses may process data in the United States and other countries. Both act under their own data-processing terms. See their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app or on this page.
